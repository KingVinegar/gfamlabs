## Purpose

Assess GFAM Labs' current app portfolio and adjacent consumer utility, education, serious-game, and entertainment opportunities. Recommend a focused near-term portfolio rather than maximizing the number of active projects.

- The owner is the final creative authority.
- The operating thesis is: pursue niches too focused to attract sustained large-player attention, but valuable enough for people to pay.

## Scope and exclusions

In scope: current-app audit, current market/competitor/pricing/channel research, opportunity gaps, revenue and maintenance assessment, and a ranked recommendation.

Out of scope: code changes, store submissions, paid campaigns, external contact, account changes, new-app implementation, and claims of user testing.

## Acceptance and evidence

Functional acceptance: every recommendation identifies an audience, paid job, distribution path, monetization fit, delivery burden, and supporting current evidence.

Experiential acceptance: the recommended product promises should be understandable as a concrete moment-of-need, and preserve the owner's preference for simple, useful, trustworthy products. This assessment does not establish actual audience comprehension or willingness to pay; that requires later validation.

Evidence: current repository/source audit, live-site inspection, public primary or authoritative sources where available, and clearly labeled inference.
