## Description (max 4000 characters)

EmberEats is a curated camping recipe app for car campers, backpackers, families, and anyone who cooks outdoors. It's organized by how you cook — not just what you eat — so you can find exactly the right recipe for the gear you have.

130+ RECIPES ACROSS 5 COOKING METHODS

Every recipe is tagged by cooking method so you know what's possible with your setup:

• Campfire — Classic open-flame cooking. Skillets, grills, and fire-roasted favorites.
• Camp Stove — Fast and reliable. From one-pot dinners to backcountry breakfasts.
• Dutch Oven — Slow-cooked camp feasts. Roasts, breads, cobblers, and stews.
• Foil Packets — Wrap it, toss it on the coals, and let it cook. Minimal cleanup.
• No-Cook — Trail mixes, wraps, snacks, and meals that need zero flame.

PLAN YOUR TRIP WITH THE TRIP LIST

Before you leave, add recipes to your Trip List. Know exactly what to cook each night and what gear to pack. The app works completely offline, so you'll have your full menu even without signal.

BUILT FOR REAL CAMPERS

Every recipe includes prep time, cook time, difficulty level, and ingredient scaling for group size. Dietary filters help you find vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, dairy-free, and nut-free options fast.

Each recipe also includes a camper's tip — practical wisdom from someone who's cooked hundreds of meals outdoors. These aren't food blog tips. They're the kind of margin notes you'd find in Grandad's camp cookbook. Real tricks that make camp cooking easier.

WORKS COMPLETELY OFFLINE

All 130+ recipes are bundled in the app. No account required. No internet needed. No subscriptions. Your camp cookbook is always with you, whether you're deep in the backcountry or at a campground with no service.

DESIGNED LIKE A COOKBOOK

The app feels like a well-loved recipe notebook — warm, handwritten, trustworthy. Serif recipe titles, parchment-colored backgrounds, and decorative dividers between sections. It's designed to feel like flipping through pages, not scrolling a feed.

FREE TO START, UNLOCK THE FULL LIBRARY

The app is free to download and includes 40+ curated recipes across all cooking methods. Unlock the full library of 130+ recipes, the Trip List planner, and the Shopping List with a single one-time purchase. Or grab just the packs you need for $0.99 each. No subscription. No recurring fees.

WHAT'S INCLUDED

• 130+ curated camping recipes
• 5 cooking methods: Campfire, Camp Stove, Dutch Oven, Foil Packets, No-Cook
• Trip List planner to organize meals before you go
• Favorites collection to save recipes you love
• Dietary filters: Vegetarian, Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Nut-Free
• Group size scaling for ingredients
• Fully offline — no internet required
• Light, readable interface designed for bright sun and headlamp use
• No account, no tracking, no complexity

PERFECT FOR

• Car campers and families planning weekend trips
• Backpackers looking for lightweight meal ideas
• Dutch oven enthusiasts mastering camp cooking
• RV and van life travelers
• Scout leaders and outdoor educators
• Anyone who wants to eat better at camp

EmberEats was built by a small team who believes camp cooking should be simple, practical, and delicious. This isn't a food blog turned app. It's a tool for people who actually cook outdoors.
